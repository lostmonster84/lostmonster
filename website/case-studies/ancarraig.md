# Ancarraig Highland Lodges

> 12 self-catering lodges overlooking Loch Ness. Custom booking system replaced expensive channel managers and eliminated double bookings entirely.

---

## The Challenge

Ancarraig Lodges had been running for 20 years, but their online presence wasn't keeping up. They were paying £500+/month for channel manager subscriptions, losing 15-20% of every booking to OTA commissions, and still dealing with double bookings because systems weren't talking to each other.

The team was spending hours every week on manual admin — updating availability across multiple platforms, copy-pasting guest details, chasing payments. Time that should've been spent improving the guest experience was being eaten by software that didn't work properly.

## What I Built

A complete direct booking website with a custom admin backend — no third-party CMS, no expensive subscriptions, no middleman taking a cut.

### Custom Booking Flow
Date selection, lodge picker, guest details, and Stripe payment — all in one smooth flow. Real-time availability means no more double bookings. Ever.

### Admin Backend
35+ admin routes for managing content, bookings, offers, and email campaigns. The team can update lodge descriptions, add seasonal offers, and manage guest communications without calling a developer.

### SEO Infrastructure
Structured data for LodgingBusiness, AggregateRating, and FAQ schema. Dynamic sitemap generation. Open Graph optimisation. The site now ranks for "Loch Ness self-catering" and "Highland lodges" — driving direct traffic instead of paying for it through OTAs.

### Email Marketing
Newsletter system with React Email templates, subscriber management, and campaign tracking. Black Friday and seasonal offer campaigns run directly from the admin panel.

## Tech Stack

- **Next.js 16** with App Router
- **Vercel Postgres** with Drizzle ORM
- **Stripe** for payments
- **Vercel Blob** for image storage
- **Resend** for transactional and marketing email
- **Framer Motion** for scroll animations
- **Playwright** for E2E testing

## The Design

Scandi-Scot Fusion — Nordic minimalism meets Highland warmth. 60-70% imagery on sales pages because the product is the landscape. Full-bleed photography with subtle parallax. Mobile-first because 70%+ of traffic comes from phones.

No gradients. No stock photos. No generic tourism template. Every image is real, every description is specific, every page is designed to convert browsers into direct bookers.

## Results

- **Zero double bookings** since launch
- **15-20% commission savings** by driving direct bookings instead of OTA referrals
- **Hours of manual admin eliminated** per week
- **Sub-1-second load times** — critical for booking conversion on mobile
- **Lighthouse scores:** Performance 95+, Accessibility 100, SEO 100
