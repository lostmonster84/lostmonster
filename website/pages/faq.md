## Working Together

### How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. A booking system starts around £5,000. A custom application starts around £10,000. A full marketplace can be £15,000+. I give fixed-price quotes after the planning phase — no hourly billing, no surprises, no scope creep.

### How long does a typical project take?

Most projects launch in 2-6 weeks. A focused booking system or landing site takes 2-3 weeks. A full SaaS application or marketplace takes 4-8 weeks. I'll give you a specific timeline in the planning phase.

### Do you work with clients outside the UK?

Yes. I'm based in Montenegro but most of my clients are in the UK. The timezone overlap (1-2 hours) means we're working the same hours. Communication is async by default — no meetings for the sake of meetings.

### How do I get started?

Send me a message through the contact form. Tell me about your business and what you need. I'll get back to you within 24 hours. If we're a good fit, we start with a discovery conversation.

### Do you take on small projects?

It depends. If it's a quick fix or a minor feature addition to an existing project, probably not — my setup is designed for building complete systems. If it's a small but well-defined project (a design system, a booking widget, a landing page), we can talk.

## Technology

### What tech stack do you use?

Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, PostgreSQL, and Stripe are my defaults. They're fast, reliable, and battle-tested across 50+ projects. I also use Cloudflare R2 for storage, Resend for email, and Claude API for AI features. If your project needs something different, I'll tell you.

### Why not WordPress / Shopify / Wix?

Those platforms are excellent for getting started. I recommend them to businesses that don't need custom functionality. But if you need something they can't do — custom pricing logic, industry-specific workflows, AI integration, multi-tenant architecture — you need a custom build.

### Will I own the code?

Yes. 100%. You get full access to the codebase, the database, and all infrastructure credentials. No lock-in, no proprietary frameworks, no "you have to stay with us." If you want to take the code and hand it to another developer tomorrow, you can.

### Can you work with my existing systems?

Usually, yes. I've integrated with Stripe, Supabase, Neon, Railway, Vercel, Google Maps, Mapbox, Twilio, various affiliate APIs, and more. If you have existing infrastructure, I'll build around it.

### How do you handle hosting and deployment?

I typically deploy to Vercel or Railway — both are reliable, fast, and cost-effective. You'll have your own account, your own credentials, full control. Monthly hosting costs are usually £0-20 for most projects. No mark-ups.

## Quality & Process

### What's the CODA framework?

It's my planning methodology: Context, Objective, Details, Acceptance. Every project goes through it before any code is written. Context = understanding your business. Objective = defining success metrics. Details = documenting every requirement. Acceptance = agreeing on "done" before starting. It prevents 90% of the problems that kill projects.

### What does "80/100 quality minimum" mean?

Every deliverable is scored against a quality checklist: brand adherence, technical quality, voice and messaging, business goals, and responsive UX. If it's below 80, it doesn't ship. This isn't marketing — it's an actual scoring system I use on every project.

### Do you test on mobile?

Everything is mobile-first. I design for phones first, then scale up to desktop. For most projects, 60-70% of traffic comes from mobile devices. Testing happens on real viewports, not just Chrome DevTools.

### What about accessibility?

WCAG 2.1 AA compliance is the minimum standard on every project. Semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, proper ARIA labels, colour contrast ratios. Accessibility isn't an add-on — it's built into the design system.

### Do you provide ongoing support?

30 days of post-launch support is included in every project. After that, I offer ongoing development at a monthly rate if you need it. Many clients come back for feature additions or new projects — but there's no obligation.

### Are you a solo developer or an agency?

Solo developer with a systematic approach. I'm not a freelancer winging it — I use documented frameworks, quality checklists, and battle-tested patterns. The advantage of working with me directly: no account managers, no telephone game, no overhead costs inflating your bill.

### How are you 70% cheaper than agencies?

No office. No middle management. No sales team. Modern development tools mean I build faster than a team of three at a traditional agency. The savings get passed to you.

### What if I'm not happy with the work?

The planning phase exists to prevent this. By the time I start building, we've agreed on exactly what you're getting. But if something genuinely isn't right, I fix it. No arguments, no change request forms. My reputation is worth more than any single project fee.

### Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, happy to sign an NDA before we discuss your project. I take confidentiality seriously — I run my own businesses and understand the value of proprietary information.

### Can you help with an existing project that another developer started?

It depends on the state of the code. I'll do a quick assessment — if the foundations are solid, I can pick it up. If they're not, I'll be honest about whether a rebuild makes more sense than a rescue. I won't charge you to pour good money after bad code.
