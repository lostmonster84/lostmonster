# How I Work

> Six phases from first conversation to launched product. No surprises, no scope creep, no crossed fingers.

---

## Phase 1: Discovery

**What happens:** I learn about your business, your customers, your problems, and your goals. Not just "what do you want?" but "why do you need it and what does success look like?"

**What you do:** Tell me about your business. Show me what's broken. Explain what you've tried. Be honest about your budget and timeline.

**What I deliver:** A clear understanding of the problem, documented in plain English. If I'm not the right fit, I'll tell you now — before either of us wastes time.

**Duration:** 1-2 days

---

## Phase 2: Planning

**What happens:** I use the CODA framework (Context, Objective, Details, Acceptance) to document everything before a single line of code is written. Every requirement, every user flow, every success metric — agreed upfront.

**What you do:** Review the plan. Challenge assumptions. Make sure I've understood your business correctly. This is the cheapest time to change direction.

**What I deliver:** A complete project plan with scope, timeline, tech stack, and acceptance criteria. You'll know exactly what you're getting, when you're getting it, and how we'll know it's done.

**Duration:** 2-3 days

---

## Phase 3: Design

**What happens:** I create the design system and key page layouts. Not a single mockup and "hope you like it" — I explore the design space and show you options that work for your business.

**What you do:** Review the designs. Pick what resonates. Give honest feedback. This is where the visual direction gets locked.

**What I deliver:** Design tokens (colours, typography, spacing), component patterns, and page layouts. A living design guide that documents every decision.

**Duration:** 3-5 days

---

## Phase 4: Build

**What happens:** I build the application using proven patterns and battle-tested frameworks. TypeScript for type safety. Next.js for performance. Tailwind for consistent styling. Every component is accessible, responsive, and tested.

**What you do:** You'll get regular updates. I'll flag decisions that need your input. No radio silence for weeks — you'll always know where things stand.

**What I deliver:** A working application, deployed to staging, that you can click through and test. Not a demo — the real thing, with real data.

**Duration:** 2-6 weeks (depends on scope)

---

## Phase 5: Quality

**What happens:** Every feature is tested against a quality checklist. Performance (Lighthouse 90+). Accessibility (WCAG AA). Mobile responsiveness (tested on real devices). Edge cases (empty states, error handling, slow connections).

Nothing ships below 80/100.

**What you do:** Test it yourself. Click everything. Try to break it. Tell me what feels wrong. This is the last chance to catch issues before launch.

**What I deliver:** A production-ready application that meets documented quality standards. Not "it works on my machine" — it works everywhere.

**Duration:** 2-3 days

---

## Phase 6: Launch & Support

**What happens:** I deploy to production, set up monitoring, and make sure everything works under real traffic. Then I stick around for 30 days to handle anything that comes up.

**What you do:** Launch. Tell your customers. Watch the metrics. Contact me if anything needs adjusting.

**What I deliver:** A live application with 30 days of post-launch support included. After that, you own everything — code, data, infrastructure. No lock-in, no ongoing fees unless you want ongoing development.

**Duration:** 1 day for launch + 30 days support

---

## What This Looks Like in Practice

| Phase | Duration | Your Time |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| Discovery | 1-2 days | 1-2 hours |
| Planning | 2-3 days | 1 hour review |
| Design | 3-5 days | 30 min review |
| Build | 2-6 weeks | Weekly check-ins |
| Quality | 2-3 days | 1 hour testing |
| Launch | 1 day + 30 days | Minimal |

**Total typical timeline:** 3-8 weeks from first conversation to live product.

**Your total time commitment:** ~5 hours across the entire project. I do the work. You make the decisions.

---

## Why This Works

Most development projects fail because of bad planning, not bad code. The CODA framework catches problems at Phase 2 — when fixing them costs hours, not weeks.

I've refined this process across 50+ projects. Every phase exists because skipping it caused problems on a real project. Nothing is theoretical. Nothing is optional.
