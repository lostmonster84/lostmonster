## My Values

**Headline:** What I Stand For

### 1. Quality Over Speed
I don't do "quick and dirty." I do "thorough and right."

**In Practice:**
- Minimum 80/100 on every deliverable
- No shortcuts on accessibility, performance, or security
- If it's below standard, it doesn't ship

**Trade-off:** I'm not the fastest developer. I'm the most reliable.

---

### 2. Systems Over Heroics
I don't rely on pulling all-nighters. I rely on proven frameworks that work every time.

**In Practice:**
- CODA planning (3-5 hours upfront saves 10+ hours in rework)
- Design Variations (5 options prevents endless revision cycles)
- Quality Checklists (catch issues before they reach you)

**Trade-off:** Less drama, more predictability.

---

### 3. Business Results Over Technical Elegance
Beautiful code matters. But conversion rates matter more.

**In Practice:**
- I measure success by business metrics (conversion, bookings, revenue)
- I optimise for users first, developers second
- I make technical decisions based on ROI, not trends

**Trade-off:** I might not use the shiniest new framework. I use what works.

### 4. Honesty Over Optimism
I'd rather lose a project than overpromise and underdeliver.

**In Practice:**
- Clear timelines (12 weeks means 12 weeks, not "hopefully 8")
- Honest about limitations ("I'm not the right fit for X")
- Transparent pricing (no hidden costs, no scope creep)

**Trade-off:** I might not tell you what you want to hear. I'll tell you what you need to know.
