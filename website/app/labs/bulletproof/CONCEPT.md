# BulletProof — Concept

> Harden your reports before reviewers run them through AI.

## The Problem

Experienced professionals (engineers, surveyors, architects, consultants) write reports based on decades of manual knowledge. Their reviewers — often less experienced but more digitally comfortable — paste these reports into AI tools and get back a list of "concerns" in 30 seconds. The reviewers don't necessarily understand the issues they're raising. They're parroting AI output.

The professional then spends hours manually defending every point. Most of it is noise.

**The asymmetry:** AI makes it trivially easy to generate criticism. Defending against it manually is an unwinnable war.

## The Solution

BulletProof gives the report sender the same force multiplier their reviewers already have.

**Two modes:**

1. **Pre-Review** — Before you send, simulate what each reviewer will flag based on their role and background. Fix the gaps or prepare responses in advance.
2. **Post-Review** — When feedback arrives, paste it in. BulletProof triages: real engineering concerns vs AI-generated noise. Draft responses backed by your expertise.

## Why This Matters

- Every industry is hitting this problem as AI adoption grows
- No tool exists that serves the report SENDER — everything helps reviewers
- The market is senior professionals (55-70+) who aren't AI users but are drowning in AI-generated criticism
- This isn't about replacing expertise — it's about giving experienced people the same tools their critics have

## Key Differentiators

1. **Reviewer personas** — Not generic AI review. Simulates YOUR actual reviewers based on their role, background, and tendencies
2. **Authority profile** — Your expertise backs every response. "I have 40 years in structural engineering" carries weight
3. **Prediction loop** — Over time, gets better at predicting what each specific reviewer will flag
4. **Built for non-technical users** — No prompts, no AI knowledge needed. Paste and click.

## Target User

- 55-70 years old
- Deep domain expertise (engineering, construction, surveying, legal, medical)
- Writes reports manually based on professional knowledge
- Reports to 3-4 reviewers who use AI to critique their work
- Not an AI user — doesn't want to be one
- Frustrated, losing patience, considering retirement over this

## Market Gap

Nothing purpose-built exists. Adjacent tools:
- Thesify — academic paper pre-review (wrong audience)
- HyperWrite — writing critique (grammar/style, not technical defence)
- Review response generators — built for customer reviews (wrong context)
- ChatGPT — requires prompt engineering skills the user doesn't have

## Name

**BulletProof** — an engineer hardens their reports before they face fire. Simple, universally understood, implies strength not weakness.
