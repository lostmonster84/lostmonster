# BulletProof — Market Analysis

> What exists, what doesn't, and why this is a gap.

---

## The Market Reality

Every AI tool in the document review space is built for **the reviewer**. Nothing is built for **the person being reviewed**. That's the gap.

---

## Adjacent Tools (Not Competitors)

| Tool | What It Does | Why It's Not This |
|---|---|---|
| [Thesify](https://www.thesify.ai/features/pre-submission-review) | Pre-submission review for academic papers | Academic-focused, not working professionals |
| [HyperWrite Writing Critique](https://www.hyperwriteai.com/aitools/writing-critique-ai) | General writing feedback | Grammar/style, not technical report defence |

| [RepBot](https://repbot.ai/ai-review-response-generator/) | AI review response generator | Built for customer reviews, wrong context |
| ChatGPT / Claude | General AI | Requires prompt engineering skills the user doesn't have |

---

## Why Nothing Exists Yet

1. **The problem is new.** AI-generated criticism only became widespread in 2024-2025. Senior professionals are just starting to feel it.
2. **The victim isn't the buyer (yet).** Tool companies build for the people adopting AI, not the people being affected by it.
3. **The user is invisible.** A 65-year-old engineer frustrated by AI nitpicking isn't on Product Hunt or Hacker News. Nobody's hearing them.
4. **It looks like a niche.** But it's not. Every industry with report submission + review cycles has this problem: engineering, construction, legal, medical, consulting, architecture, surveying.

## Market Size

### Who Has This Problem

Any professional who:
- Writes reports based on expertise
- Submits them to reviewers
- Where reviewers have access to AI tools

| **Legal** | Case summaries, compliance reports, due diligence | Submitted to partners, courts, clients |
| **Medical** | Clinical reports, case notes, research papers | Submitted to boards, journals, regulators |
| **Architecture** | Design rationales, planning submissions | Submitted to councils, clients, consultants |
| **Consulting** | Strategy reports, audit findings | Submitted to clients, boards |
| **Surveying** | Valuation reports, condition surveys | Submitted to clients, lenders, solicitors |

### The Trend

- AI adoption among reviewers is accelerating
- The gap between "AI-assisted critique" and "manual defence" widens every month
- Senior professionals are the most affected — they have the most expertise but the least AI fluency
- Some are retiring early because of this frustration

---

## Competitive Moat

1. **Reviewer personas are proprietary.** The more a user builds out their reviewers, the harder it is to switch.
2. **Prediction accuracy improves with data.** Each review cycle makes the system smarter about what specific reviewers flag.
3. **Authority profiles compound.** The user's expertise library grows over time, making responses stronger.
4. **Network effects.** If multiple people in the same industry use it, the system learns what certain job roles typically flag across organisations.
5. **Trust.** The first tool to earn trust from non-technical professionals wins. This audience doesn't tool-hop.

## Pricing Considerations (TBD)

### Who Pays?

| Model | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Individual** (£10-20/month) | Low friction, direct relationship | Harder to justify, personal expense |
| **Company** (£50-200/month) | Higher value, business expense | Harder sell, procurement involved |
| **Per-report** (£2-5/report) | Pay for what you use, easy to trial | Unpredictable revenue |

### Free Tier
Essential for adoption. Must allow:
- Paste a report, get one free review
- See enough value to justify paying
- No signup required for first use (NIGELX condition)

## Go-to-Market

### Phase 1: One User
- Build for James's friend specifically
- One industry (engineering), one workflow, one person
- Validate the core loop works

### Phase 2: Word of Mouth
- Engineer tells 5 engineer mates
- Built-in sharing: "Here's what BulletProof flagged on my report"
- Target LinkedIn posts from frustrated professionals

### Phase 3: Industry Expansion
- Construction → Legal → Medical → Consulting
- Each industry gets tailored reviewer persona templates
- Industry-specific knowledge bases for smarter flagging
