## The Problem

You're paying £500+/month for a channel manager that still causes double bookings. You're handing 15-20% of every booking to OTAs. Your availability calendar doesn't sync. Your staff spend hours on manual admin that software should handle.

Off-the-shelf booking tools work for most businesses. But if you've outgrown them — or you're tired of paying monthly for features you don't use — you need something built for how your business actually operates.

## What I Build

Complete booking systems tailored to your specific operation. Not a template with your logo on it — a system designed around your properties, your pricing model, your guest journey.

### Real-Time Availability
Calendar-based availability that updates instantly. No sync delays, no double bookings, no "sorry, that one's actually taken." Guests see what's available right now and book it in seconds.

### Payment Processing
Stripe-powered payments with deposits, full payments, or custom split options. Automatic receipts. Refund handling. Currency support for international guests. PCI compliant without the headache.

### Admin Dashboard
Manage bookings, update availability, create seasonal offers, and communicate with guests — all from one place. No switching between three different platforms. No developer needed for day-to-day changes.

### Direct Booking Optimisation
SEO infrastructure that drives organic traffic directly to your booking page. Structured data for Google. Optimised checkout flow. Every decision designed to convert browsers into bookers — without paying an OTA for the privilege.

### Email Automation
Booking confirmations, pre-arrival information, post-stay follow-ups, and marketing campaigns. React Email templates that match your brand. Newsletter management with subscriber preferences.
