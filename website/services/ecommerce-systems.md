## The Problem

You're paying platform fees on every transaction. Your store looks like every other store on the same template. Your checkout flow loses customers at the payment step. You can't customise the parts that actually matter for your business without hiring a Shopify expert at £150/hour.

Generic e-commerce platforms are brilliant for getting started. But when your business has specific needs — custom pricing models, B2B workflows, subscription products, multi-currency — the limitations start costing you money.

## What I Build

E-commerce systems designed around how your business actually sells. Not a generic storefront with plugins bolted on — a purpose-built sales machine.

### Product Management
Whatever your catalogue looks like — simple products, configurable options, subscription tiers, B2B pricing, seasonal availability. Your admin panel lets you manage everything without touching code.

### Conversion-Optimised Checkout
Every step tested and refined. Guest checkout prominent (60% recovery rate). Mobile-optimised (because that's where your customers are). Transparent pricing with no surprises at the last step. Stripe-powered payments.

### Customer Accounts
Order history, saved addresses, subscription management, wishlist. The features your customers actually use — not 50 settings they'll never touch.

### Search and Filtering
Smart product search with filters that make sense for your catalogue. Category pages, tag-based browsing, price ranges, availability. Instant results, no page reloads.

### Admin Dashboard
Manage orders, products, inventory, customers, and promotions from one dashboard. No switching between platforms. Bulk actions, quick edits, and real-time analytics.
