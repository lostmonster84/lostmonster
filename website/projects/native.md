# Native Automotive — Independent Land Rover Specialist

Independent Land Rover specialist garage providing servicing, repairs, modifications, rust prevention, and ECU remapping in the Scottish Highlands.

## What It Does

Physical garage business (not a software project). Born from the need to maintain the WildTrax Land Rover rental fleet in-house. Now serves external customers from across Scotland.

## Business Details

- **Founded:** 2019
- **Location:** Hatton Croft, Forres, Scotland (recently relocated to larger workshop)
- **Service area:** Scottish Highlands — customers travel from Edinburgh and Dundee
- **Services:** Land Rover servicing, repairs, custom modifications, rust prevention, ECU remapping

## Team

- **Owner:** James (100% equity)
- **Managing Director / Lead Mechanic:** Ben (equity partnership under discussion)
- Growing team with new hires and apprenticeships

## Ecosystem Connection

Part of James's interconnected business ecosystem:
