# Native Automotive

> Independent Land Rover specialist garage in the Scottish Highlands. The portfolio's only physical business, and its steadiest non-seasonal cashflow.

**Status:** Non-code business
**URL:** https://www.nativeautomotive.co.uk
**Industry:** Automotive / Specialist Vehicle Repair & Modification
**Repo:** /Volumes/Projects/native _(no codebase - the directory holds a single image asset only)_
**Last updated:** 2026-05-14

## What it is

Native Automotive is a physical garage business, the only venture in the portfolio that is not a software product. It is an independent Land Rover specialist providing servicing, repairs, custom modifications, rust prevention and ECU remapping at Hatton Croft, Forres, in the Scottish Highlands. Real workshop, real tools, real vehicles on ramps.

## Origin story

Native exists because of a problem WildTrax had. The WildTrax Land Rover rental fleet needed reliable, specialist maintenance, and depending on outside garages for it was a risk and a cost. Building the capability in-house made more sense, so a garage was created. Founded in 2019, it quickly outgrew that internal remit: it now serves external customers from across Scotland, with people travelling from Edinburgh and Dundee for specialist Land Rover work. It recently relocated to a larger workshop. The problem it solves is twofold: practically, it keeps the WildTrax fleet running; strategically, it gives the portfolio a non-seasonal, real-world cashflow business that does not depend on tourism, software or a launch date.

## Customers

Land Rover owners across the Scottish Highlands and beyond - the specialist niche means customers travel two hours or more to use it. Services: servicing, repairs, custom modifications, rust prevention, ECU remapping. Internally, it also maintains the entire WildTrax rental fleet (the anchor customer).

## How it works

A conventional specialist garage operation: bookings, workshop time, parts, labour, MOT and servicing cycles. The strategic mechanism is the ecosystem link: it was born from WildTrax fleet maintenance, continues to service that fleet, and operates independently and profitably for external customers at the same time. It currently runs without a booking system or meaningful online presence - that gap is the clearest growth opportunity.

## Tech stack

None - this is a physical business. There is a marketing website at nativeautomotive.co.uk; the `/Volumes/Projects/native` folder contains only an image asset, no application. A customer-facing booking system and improved web presence are noted as an open opportunity, and would be the natural first point for The Firm crew to get involved.

## Monetisation

Direct garage revenue: servicing, repairs, modifications, remapping, rust prevention, billed by labour and parts. The defining commercial feature is that it is non-seasonal and consistent, which makes it the steadiest earner of James's three Scottish businesses, in deliberate contrast to tourism-dependent WildTrax and Ancarraig. Higher-margin work (modifications, rust prevention, ECU remapping) sits alongside steady servicing cycles and the WildTrax fleet-maintenance anchor.

## Status & scale

Active and trading. The most consistent revenue stream of the three Scottish businesses. Recently moved to a larger workshop (physical evidence of growth and reinvestment). Workforce expansion in progress, with new hires and apprenticeships. Estimated small-to-medium specialist garage, 5+ years trading.

## Team

- **James** - owner, 100% equity.
- **Ben** - Managing Director / Lead Mechanic; runs day-to-day operations and the complex technical work. Equity partnership under discussion.
- A growing team with new hires and apprentices.
- No AI agents involved - this is a hands-on physical operation, and The Firm crew is deliberately not used here.

## Agent-first angle

Native Automotive's role is to be the deliberate non-AI, non-software node in the portfolio, and that is precisely its strategic value. While the rest of the portfolio tests how far an AI crew can build and run software businesses, Native provides the counterweight: non-seasonal, recession-resistant, real-world cashflow and a physical operational base that anchors the more experimental ventures. The agent-first angle here is the absence of one, on purpose. If and when a booking system or customer portal is built for it, that becomes the entry point for The Firm - but the business itself is people, tools and trust.

## Strategic position

- **Role in the portfolio:** The ballast. Native is the stable, tangible, non-seasonal business that offsets the risk profile of everything else. Doma, Evidis, GoVozi and StayFlo are bets at various stages; Native is a profitable garage with customers today. It makes the portfolio fundable and survivable.
- **Where the leverage is:** Two clear levers, both off-software. First, capacity - workforce expansion (hires, apprenticeships) converts directly into revenue in a way software ventures do not. Second, the missing web/booking layer - it currently has no booking system; adding one is a small, high-confidence project that would lift utilisation and is the one place the AI crew could obviously help.
- **Push or park:** RUN and resource. The steady earner - keep it well-staffed and consider giving it the light digital layer it lacks. Low risk, real return.
- **Moat:** A specialist niche reputation, geographic pull (customers travelling from Edinburgh and Dundee), the in-house relationship with the WildTrax fleet, and the simple fact that skilled Land Rover specialists are scarce in the Highlands.
- **Biggest risk / next bottleneck:** Key-person dependency on Ben as lead mechanic, with an equity partnership still under discussion, and skilled-labour supply in a rural area. Growth is gated on people, not demand.

## Open questions / risks

- Key-person dependency on Ben; equity partnership still under discussion (terms, vesting, succession).
- Skilled-labour supply in a rural area - growth is gated on hiring and apprenticeships.
- No booking system, limited web footprint - a competitive gap vs larger garages.
- WildTrax dependency: unknown what share of revenue is fleet maintenance; if WildTrax's fleet winds down (it is being shed by 30 June 2026), Native loses an anchor customer and a referral loop.
- Outside James's software wheelhouse - needs management attention as a physical/people business, not a codebase.
