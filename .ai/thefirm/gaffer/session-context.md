## Design Decisions Made
- No em dashes in shipped content. Ever. Across all projects. Codified in PROTOCOL.md Universal Copy Rules, WORDX Punctuation Rules, and slop test Red Flag #11
- "2-4 weeks" timeline claim removed. Can't guarantee timelines when workload varies. Replaced with verifiable metrics (80/100 floor, 4.9/5 rating)
- Never claim outcomes we don't control: "real users", "real revenue", "making money" are client outcomes, not ours. We claim build quality, track record, process
- Dev studio voice: punchy, honest, no marketing-speak. "Get in touch" not "Start a Conversation". "See the work" not "Let's Build Something"
- CTAs must be confident: "Start a project" tested well. Need strong filled CTA with glow, not passive outlined buttons
- Every hero needs: hook (attention) + scope (interest) + visible proof (desire) + bold CTA (action). Missing any one tanks the AIDAX score

## Rejected Alternatives
- "We Build What We Sell" as hero: identity statement, not value proposition. Tells visitor who YOU are, not what THEY get
- Specific timeline promises: dishonest when workload varies
- "Real users, real revenue" claims: not in our control, applies to our products not client builds
- "Nothing ships below 80" as standalone hero: intriguing but "80" means nothing without context. Needs proof bar backup
- "We build software" as hero: too generic. Everyone builds software
